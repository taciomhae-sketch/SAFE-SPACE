Safe Space promotes psychological safety, emotional well-being, and mutual respect for all students.

Safe Space Community Guidelines

1. Empathy & Mutual Respect: Treat every student with dignity. Personal insults, harassment, and targeted attacks are strictly prohibited.

2. Absolute Safety First: Content encouraging self-harm, suicide, or physical violence is not permitted on this platform. Immediate safety guidance and hotlines are provided.

3. Safe Emotional Expression: Expressing sadness, anxiety, burnout, or academic distress is always welcomed and supported. Personal struggles will never be penalized.

4. Constructive Peer Support: We encourage uplifting comments, compassionate listening, and healthy coping suggestions.

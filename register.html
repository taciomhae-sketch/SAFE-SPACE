Safe Space Privacy Notice (RA 10173)

Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012) Compliance Notice:
Notice Version: 1.0 | Effective Date: November 2026

A. Personal Information Collected

Safe Space collects only information strictly necessary for the platform's actual educational, student verification, and safe-expression purposes. We collect:

  • Email address: For account authentication, notifications, and verification.
  • Username: For displaying your chosen identity within the student platform.
  • Student ID number: For verifying legitimate enrollment in the educational institution.
  • Student ID image: Captured via camera or file upload, used exclusively to verify that the registered student holds a valid school-issued ID.
  • Password: Stored strictly using irreversible, secure password hashing (passwords are never stored as plain text and cannot be viewed by administrators).
  • Account registration info: First name, last name, birthday (used to calculate age), and sex/gender.
  • Profile information: Voluntarily provided avatar selection or bio.
  • Community posts and interactions: Posts, comments, reactions, and saved items created by you.
  • Reports: Reports submitted regarding community guidelines or safety concerns.
  • AI moderation & sentiment analysis results: Real-time text safety classifications generated by the client-side/moderation engine.
  • Security & access logs: Login timestamps, privacy acknowledgment records, and audit events necessary for account safety.

We do not request unnecessary sensitive information. We collect only what is lawful, proportional, and directly relevant to platform operations.

B. Purpose of Data Collection

Safe Space processes personal information for the following specific, legitimate purposes:

  1. Creating and managing student accounts.
  2. Authenticating users during login.
  3. Checking Student ID number consistency between entered text and school documents.
  4. Supporting student verification and ensuring campus safety.
  5. Maintaining account security and preventing unauthorized access.
  6. Moderating community posts to detect potentially harmful, offensive, or distressing content.
  7. Supporting student safety through automated crisis helpline recommendations (e.g., NCMH 1553, 911).
  8. Managing reports and platform incidents fairly and confidentially.
  9. Improving platform functionality, peer support, and user experience.
  10. Maintaining necessary system audit records.

Notice on AI Moderation: Safe Space utilizes AI moderation and sentiment analysis to flag cyberbullying, self-harm risks, and harassment. AI moderation is an assistive safety filter and cannot guarantee absolute student safety or replace human counseling.

C. Student ID Image Processing

When you register, you submit an image of your school-issued Student ID via device camera or file upload. The system utilizes optical character recognition (OCR) to extract the ID number and verify consistency against your form input.

  • Verification limitation: OCR consistency matching confirms document number alignment but does not independently certify document authenticity.
  • Storage & Protection: Uploaded Student ID documents are stored in protected, access-restricted cloud storage and local encrypted cache.
  • Access restriction: Student ID documents are accessible strictly by authorized institutional administrators for verification audits and are never displayed publicly or to other students.
  • Retention & Disposal: Student ID images are retained only for the duration of the student's active enrollment or the research evaluation period, after which they are securely purged according to institutional retention policies.

D. Data Storage and Security Measures

Safe Space implements organizational, physical, and technical security safeguards, including:

  • Secure token-based authentication and sessions.
  • Irreversible bcrypt/argon password hashing.
  • Row-Level Security (RLS) policies restricting database queries to authorized users.
  • HTTPS encrypted in-transit communication and protected storage buckets.
  • Role-based administrative access controls.

Security Notice: While we implement robust industry safeguards, no digital system can claim absolute invulnerability. We continually review and update our safeguards to protect student data.

E. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Safe Space does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Personal data is shared only with:

  • Authorized school counselors / administrators: Exclusively for student verification, critical safety escalation, or managing incident reports.
  • Essential technical service providers: Secure cloud database and hosting providers operating under strict data processing agreements.
  • Legal obligations: When required by lawful court order, regulation, or immediate protection of human life.

F. Your Rights as a Data Subject

Under Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012), you are entitled to the following rights:

  • Right to be Informed: To know whether your personal data is being processed.
  • Right to Access: To request a copy of your personal data held in Safe Space.
  • Right to Rectification: To correct inaccurate or outdated information via Profile settings.
  • Right to Erasure or Blocking: To request deletion or suspension of your account and personal data, subject to legitimate safety/legal retention requirements.
  • Right to Object: To object to data processing where applicable by law.
  • Right to Data Portability: To obtain an electronic copy of your submitted information.
  • Right to File a Complaint: To lodge a formal complaint with the National Privacy Commission (NPC) at https://privacy.gov.ph if your rights have been violated.

G. Data Retention Policy

Personal data is retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill the educational, safety, and community purposes outlined in this notice. When accounts are deleted or retention periods lapse, records are systematically anonymized or destroyed, except where specific records must be preserved for documented legal, safety, or disciplinary audits.

Safe Space Terms and Conditions

Safe Space Community Rules:
Terms Version 1.0 | Platform Usage Agreement

1. Eligibility & Accurate Information

Safe Space is designed for students. By creating an account, you agree that you are a bona fide student and have submitted your actual school Student ID.

2. Community Conduct & Respect

Our platform thrives on mutual empathy, psychological safety, and respect. You agree NOT to post:

  • Bullying, harassment, hate speech, or targeted defamation.
  • Direct threats of physical violence against any person.
  • Content encouraging suicide, self-harm, or harmful acts.
  • Nudity, sexual solicitation, or inappropriate commercial spam.

3. Content Moderation & Enforcement

Posts and comments are subject to automatic AI moderation and human counselor review. Content violating critical safety rules will be blocked. Off-words are automatically masked with asterisks, and emotionally sensitive posts are blurred to protect readers.

4. Anonymous Posting Responsibility

Anonymous posting allows you to seek advice without fear of judgment. While your username is hidden from other students, abusive behavior remains traceable by administrators to ensure community safety.

5. Educational & Peer Support Notice

Safe Space provides peer support, venting spaces, and wellness resources. It is not a clinical medical facility or emergency response station. In life-threatening emergencies, students must contact official hotlines (NCMH 1553, 911).

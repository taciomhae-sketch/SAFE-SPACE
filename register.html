Safe Space Privacy Policy & Terms

1. Student Identity & Account Information

Safe Space collects basic registration information: your name, birthday (used to derive age), gender identity, username, email address, and securely hashed password. Profile photos or avatar selections are optional.

2. Purpose of Collection

Your information is used strictly to provide access to Safe Space support features, maintain respectful community moderation, enable confidential communication, and preserve account security.

3. Account Security & Passwords

Passwords are securely hashed before storage and are never stored as plain text. Passwords cannot be viewed by platform administrators or sent back to the browser.

4. Anonymous & Safe Expression

When you post anonymously in community spaces, your personal identifying information is hidden from peer students. Your well-being, privacy, and peace of mind are paramount.

5. AI Moderation & Safety

Safe Space utilizes respectful AI moderation to safeguard students against cyberbullying, self-harm risks, and harassment. Content flagged for safety review is handled confidentially.

6. User Rights, Profile & Data Deletion

You have full autonomy over your data. You can view, edit, or remove your personal details anytime via Profile → Account Information. You may also request account deactivation or deletion.

7. Non-Medical Platform Notice

Safe Space is a peer support and safe-expression web platform for students. It does not replace professional medical advice, clinical psychiatric diagnosis, or emergency crisis intervention services.

8. Contact & Research Context

Safe Space is crafted for student well-being as part of an academic thesis initiative. For inquiries, reach out to the Safe Space Student Support research team.
